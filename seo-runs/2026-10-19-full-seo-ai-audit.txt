Viby — Full SEO & AI Search Audit
joinviby.co.il | October 19, 2026 | Israeli market | Google Search, AI search, and organic customer acquisition
Executive assessment
Viby has a promising technical SEO foundation, but its biggest growth opportunity is to become discoverable for more than digital punch cards.

The live homepage currently has a valid HTTP 200 response, Hebrew RTL markup, indexable robots directives, descriptive metadata, social sharing tags, and a publicly accessible XML sitemap. These are positive starting points.

However, the homepage is positioned almost exclusively around כרטיסייה דיגיטלית לעסק, while Viby provides a much broader collection of business tools: customer loyalty, customer retention, digital wallets, Google review tools, NFC customer engagement, and Viby UP.

That creates a strategic challenge: a business owner searching for "מערכת מועדון לקוחות", "איך להחזיר לקוחות לעסק", or "איך להשיג יותר ביקורות בגוגל" may be an ideal Viby prospect without searching for a digital punch card.

The SEO goal should be to make Viby discoverable at three levels:
1. Product discovery: People actively searching for digital punch cards, loyalty systems, NFC signs, review tools, and digital wallets.
2. Problem discovery: Business owners looking for ways to increase repeat purchases, collect Google reviews, retain customers, and grow revenue.
3. AI recommendation discovery: People asking ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Perplexity, and similar services to recommend the best tools for their businesses.
This report distinguishes live-verified findings from hypotheses that require Search Console, analytics, or a deeper crawler-based inspection. It does not treat an attractive keyword as proof of high search volume or claim a Google ranking without a defensible measurement.

1.2 Critical opportunity: internal linking
High priority
The product pages use a service-switching experience. But in the fetched page markup, I found very few normal, crawlable links to the other product pages.

For example, /viby-tap exposes links to the homepage, its own section, terms, and privacy. It does not expose an obvious network of standard links to all the other major Viby products in the extracted links.

Likewise, /smart-wheel and /digital-wallet appear to rely heavily on the same service-switching presentation.

This matters because Google uses links to discover pages and understand their relationships. Links expressed only as JavaScript click actions are less reliable than normal HTML anchors. 

developers.google.com
+1




Recommendation: Keep the existing service switcher, but ensure that each product has an actual <a href="..."> link. Add a semantically clear product navigation section, and link related services contextually inside page copy.

For instance, the digital punch-card page should naturally reference the digital wallet, Viby UP, and the smart wheel in relevant sections. The Viby UP page should link to the Google review NFC solution, and vice versa.

6. AI SEO / Generative Engine Optimization
This deserves its own strategy.

The goal is not merely to appear in Google. It is to become an understandable, credible, and relevant candidate when someone asks an AI assistant:
מה המערכת הכי טובה למועדון לקוחות לבית קפה קטן בישראל?

Or:
איזו מערכת מאפשרת כרטיסיות ניקוב ב־Apple Wallet בלי להוריד אפליקציה?

We cannot guarantee that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Google AI will rank or recommend Viby first. Their retrieval and ranking systems differ and can change.

Google's own guidance for AI search emphasizes original, helpful content and established search best practices rather than a separate secret AI optimization technique. 

developers.google.com



6.1 Create extractable, direct answers
Each product page should contain an early section that describes the product in one or two factual paragraphs, without requiring a visitor to understand the brand name first.

For example:
Suggested answer-first content

מהי מערכת כרטיסיות דיגיטליות לעסקים?

מערכת כרטיסיות דיגיטליות מאפשרת לעסק לתגמל לקוחות על רכישות וביקורים חוזרים באמצעות כרטיס נאמנות בטלפון.

Viby היא מערכת ישראלית המאפשרת ללקוחות להצטרף בסריקת QR, לצבור ניקובים ולשמור כרטיסייה ממותגת ב־Apple Wallet או Google Wallet, ללא צורך בהורדת אפליקציית Viby ללקוחות.

This content is easy for both people and retrieval systems to interpret.
6.2 Strengthen Viby as an identifiable entity
At present, product names dominate the website experience. Viby also needs a strong company-level explanation.

Create an /about page with:
- What Viby is and which businesses it serves.
- Which products it provides and how they fit together.
- The actual company or operator identity and service area.
- Verified customer examples.
- Official contact channels.
- The relationship between Viby, Viby UP, Viby Tap, and Viby Rate.
- Real photography of the product and company when available.
Use Organization schema with consistent branding and links to authentic official profiles.

This does not create an AI knowledge panel automatically, but it helps reduce ambiguity about the brand.
6.3 Build original evidence
The website already has a strong asset: a visible customer-logo section containing numerous local businesses.

That is more useful than merely repeating keyword-rich sentences.

Turn some of these relationships into real, approved customer stories:

Example structure:
- Business type and challenge.
- Why the owner adopted Viby.
- Which Viby tool they selected.
- What the customer experiences.
- What changed operationally.
- Actual before-and-after numbers, only when measured and permissioned.
- Real photographs or screenshots.
A story supported by first-hand evidence is much more useful than a generic claim that Viby increases retention.
6.4 Build external credibility
Viby should establish relevant brand mentions and earned links beyond its own website.

Good opportunities include partnerships with small-business consultants, cafe-industry publications, relevant local business communities, merchant software directories, and approved customer case studies.

Avoid buying low-quality backlink packages, mass-producing guest posts, or creating fake reviews.
6.5 AI crawler discoverability
As part of a technical review, verify that important public pages can be accessed by relevant crawlers, including Googlebot and applicable AI search crawlers.

Do not blindly allow every bot, because some crawlers are used for model training rather than search discovery. Maintain an explicit policy aligned with V iby's privacy, infrastructure, and content preferences.

The current public robots.txt has a broad allow rule and excludes /api/, which is a sensible base pattern. Additional policies should be introduced only after understanding the specific crawler behavior and what Viby wants to permit.

I would monitor the same 10–20 questions repeatedly across supported AI-search products. For example:
